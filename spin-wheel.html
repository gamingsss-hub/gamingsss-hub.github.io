What is Quest Wheel?

Quest Wheel is a creative adventure generator that combines three random selections into one unique quest. Discover a realm, receive a mission and uncover a surprise twist. Every completed combination creates a new fictional adventure.

How to Play

  • Spin the first wheel to discover your adventure realm.
  • Move to the second stage and reveal your mission.
  • Spin the final wheel to reveal an unexpected twist.
  • View your completed quest and copy or save it in your history.
  • Customize all three wheels to create your own combinations.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is Quest Wheel free?

Yes. You can use all three wheels and customize your options for free.

2. Can I create my own wheel options?

Yes. Add your own realm, mission and twist options using the customization panel.

3. Does Quest Wheel work on mobile?

Yes. The layout adapts to smartphones, tablets and desktop screens.

4. Where is my quest history stored?

Your recent quest history is saved in your browser's local storage. It does not automatically sync across devices.
