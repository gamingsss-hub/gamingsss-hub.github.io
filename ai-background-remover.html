Frequently Asked Questions

Is this AI background remover free?

Toolora AI does not charge you to use this page. The AI model is downloaded to your browser and processing uses your device's resources and internet connection.

Does the result have a transparent background?

Yes. The tool produces a PNG image with the background removed and transparency preserved.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

The selected image is passed to the in-browser removal model. It is not uploaded to Toolora AI's server. The model files themselves are fetched from the model provider.

Why does the first removal take longer?

The browser needs to download the AI model and supporting files the first time. Later processing may be faster if those files remain cached.

Which image formats are supported?

This page accepts JPG, JPEG, PNG and WebP images up to 12 MB. The processed result is downloaded as PNG.
