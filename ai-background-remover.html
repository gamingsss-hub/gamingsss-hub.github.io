What is a transparent PNG?

PNG is an image format that supports transparency. Unlike a standard image with a solid background, a transparent PNG can have invisible areas. This makes it useful when placing a subject on a different background in a presentation, design or document.

Which images work best?

Images with a clearly visible subject and reasonable contrast between the subject and its background can be easier for an AI model to process. Good lighting and sharp image details may also help. Very busy backgrounds, motion blur or subjects that blend into the background can affect the result.

Supported image formats and file size

  • JPG and JPEG: commonly used for photographs.
  • PNG: supports transparency and is commonly used for graphics.
  • WebP: a modern image format supported by many browsers.
  • Maximum input file size: 12 MB.
  • Output format: PNG.
