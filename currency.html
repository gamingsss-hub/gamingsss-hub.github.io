🌍 About Currency Converter

Toolora AI Currency Converter helps you calculate the value of one currency in another. Select your currencies, enter an amount and get an estimated converted value using the latest available exchange-rate data from the API.

✨ How to Use

  • Enter the amount you want to convert.
  • Select the currency you are converting from.
  • Select your target currency.
  • Press the Convert Currency button.
  • Use the swap button to reverse the currencies.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is this currency converter free?

Yes. You can use this converter for free without creating an account.

2. Are the exchange rates updated?

Rates are fetched from an external exchange-rate API and are generally updated daily. They may not match the exact rate offered by banks or payment providers.

3. Does it support USD to INR?

Yes. You can convert USD to INR, INR to USD and many other supported currencies.

4. Can I use it on mobile?

Yes. The converter is designed to work on mobile phones, tablets and desktop devices.
