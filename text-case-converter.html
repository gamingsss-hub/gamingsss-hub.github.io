What Is a Text Case Converter?

A text case converter is an online utility that changes the capitalization of letters in your text. Instead of manually editing every word, you can paste your content and choose a case format with one click.

Different Text Case Formats

  • UPPERCASE: Converts all applicable letters into capital letters.
  • lowercase: Changes all applicable letters into small letters.
  • Title Case: Capitalizes the first letter of each word.
  • Sentence Case: Capitalizes the first letter of the text.
  • Toggle Case: Changes uppercase letters to lowercase and lowercase letters to uppercase.
