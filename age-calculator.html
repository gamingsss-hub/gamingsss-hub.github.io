Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is this age calculator free?

Yes. Toolora AI's age calculator is free to use and does not require an account.

2. How is exact age calculated?

The calculator compares your date of birth with today's date and calculates completed calendar years, months and remaining days.

3. Does it support leap year birthdays?

Yes. The calculator handles February 29 birthdays. In non-leap years, the birthday countdown uses February 28 as the birthday date.

4. What is total days lived?

It is the number of complete calendar days between your date of birth and the current date.

5. Is my date of birth stored?

No. The calculation happens in your browser. This page does not send your date of birth to a server.

6. What does the zodiac sign mean?

The zodiac sign shown is based on traditional Western astrology date ranges. It is provided for general interest.
