About World GK Quiz

World GK Quiz is a free online general knowledge game designed to challenge your understanding of countries, capitals, continents, oceans, currencies, international organizations, world history and science. Each round contains 15 randomly selected questions.

How to Play

Choose one of four difficulty levels. Read each question and select the correct answer from four options before the timer ends. Earn points for correct answers, build consecutive-answer streaks and use lifelines when you need help.

Scoring System

Easy questions award 10 points, Medium 20, Hard 30 and Expert 40. Correct answers in a streak of three or more earn an additional bonus. Wrong answers and lifelines reduce your score.
