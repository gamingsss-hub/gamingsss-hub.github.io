Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is the World Clock showing real time?

Yes. Each city clock uses the current device time and its official IANA time-zone identifier. The display updates every second.

2. Does this World Clock need an API?

No external time API is required. The browser's built-in JavaScript internationalization features provide the time-zone calculations.

3. What is a time zone?

A time zone is a region that follows a particular standard time. Different cities may have different UTC offsets.

4. What is daylight saving time?

Some regions adjust their clocks seasonally. The browser's time-zone database accounts for these changes for supported zones.

5. Can I save favourite cities?

Yes. Use the star button on a city card. Your favourites are saved in your browser on that device.

6. Is the World Clock free?

Yes. Toolora AI World Clock is free to use and does not require an account.
