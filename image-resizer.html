What Is an Image Resizer?

An image resizer is a tool that changes the width and height of a digital image. Resizing can help prepare pictures for websites, social media, presentations, documents and other digital platforms.

Toolora AI Image Resizer allows you to adjust image dimensions, choose popular presets and export your image in JPG, PNG or WEBP format. Image processing takes place locally in your browser.

Why Resize Images?

Different platforms and applications may require different image dimensions. Resizing helps prepare an image for a specific layout and can reduce its pixel dimensions. It is useful for website graphics, profile pictures, thumbnails and presentations.

Understanding Image Formats

  • JPG: Commonly used for photographs and smaller image files.
  • PNG: Useful for graphics that need transparency.
  • WEBP: A modern format that supports lossy and lossless compression.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is Toolora Image Resizer free?

Yes. You can resize images using this browser-based tool without a paid subscription.

2. Which image formats are supported?

You can upload JPG, PNG and WEBP images and export the resized result in these formats.

3. Will resizing reduce image quality?

Changing dimensions or using lossy formats can affect image quality. The quality setting helps control JPG and WEBP export quality.

4. What does Keep Aspect Ratio mean?

It keeps the original width-to-height proportion when you change one dimension, helping prevent unwanted stretching.

5. Are my uploaded images sent to a server?

This tool processes images locally in your browser. The image is not uploaded to a Toolora server by this page.

6. Can I resize images for Instagram and YouTube?

Yes. The quick presets include common square, portrait, story and YouTube thumbnail dimensions.

7. Can I enlarge a small image?

Yes, but enlarging an image does not restore missing detail. The result may look less sharp than the original.
