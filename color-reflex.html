About Color Reflex Game

Color Reflex is a free browser-based brain and reaction game. It challenges you to identify colors quickly while paying attention to the instructions. Different difficulty levels add extra visual distractions to make the challenge more interesting.

How to Play

  • Select a game duration and difficulty.
  • Read the instruction displayed above the color word.
  • Select the correct answer from the four color buttons.
  • Try to build a streak by answering correctly.
  • Complete the timer and review your results.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Color Reflex free?

Yes. You can play this game for free without creating an account.

Does it work on mobile phones?

Yes. The layout and answer buttons adapt to mobile, tablet and desktop screens. Touch and mouse input are supported.

What is Hard mode?

Hard mode uses mismatched word and ink colors along with a changing background. Follow the instruction carefully to choose the correct answer.

Is my best score saved?

Your best score is saved locally in your browser. It does not automatically sync between different devices.
