Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is Toolora AI Word Counter free?

Yes. You can use this online word counter for free without creating an account.

2. How are words counted?

Words are counted by separating text wherever whitespace occurs. Multiple spaces and line breaks are treated as separators.

3. Does the character count include spaces?

Yes. The Characters statistic includes spaces and punctuation. The No-Space Characters statistic excludes whitespace.

4. How is reading time calculated?

Reading time is estimated using approximately 200 words per minute. Actual reading speed varies from person to person.

5. Is my text uploaded to a server?

No. The counting functions run directly in your browser. This page does not send your text to a server.

6. Can I download my text?

Yes. Select Download TXT to save your entered text as a plain text file.
