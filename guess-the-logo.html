About Guess The Logo

Guess The Logo is a free online brand recognition game. Identify popular company logos from different industries, including technology, entertainment, shopping and social media. Challenge yourself with three difficulty levels and improve your visual memory.

How to Play

Select Easy, Medium or Hard mode. Study the displayed logo and select the correct brand from four options before the timer ends. Use hints and the 50:50 lifeline strategically. Correct answers earn points, while lifelines and skipped questions reduce your score.
