About Country & Capital Quiz

The Country Capital Quiz is a free geography game that tests your knowledge of countries and their capital cities. Explore capitals from Asia, Europe, Africa, North America, South America and Oceania. Each round contains 15 randomly selected questions.

How to Play

Select a difficulty level and start the quiz. You will see a country and four possible capital cities. Choose the correct answer before the countdown reaches zero. Correct answers earn points, while lifelines can help you when you get stuck.

Difficulty Levels

Easy introduces well-known capitals. Medium covers a wider range of countries. Hard and Expert include less familiar capital cities and shorter response times.
