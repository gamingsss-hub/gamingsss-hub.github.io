About Aim Trainer

Toolora AI Aim Trainer is a free browser-based reaction and precision game. Targets appear at different positions inside the practice arena. Hit them as quickly and accurately as possible before the timer ends.

How to Play

  • Select a game duration: 15, 30 or 60 seconds.
  • Choose Easy, Medium or Hard difficulty.
  • Press Start Game to begin.
  • Click or tap the targets as they appear.
  • Try to achieve a high score while maintaining accuracy.
  • Check your results and personal best after the session.

Mobile and Desktop Support

The game supports mouse clicks on desktop and touch input on mobile devices. The practice arena automatically adjusts to the available screen size.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Aim Trainer free?

Yes. This browser game is free to play and does not require registration.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. You can play using your phone's touchscreen or a desktop mouse.

Is my best score saved?

Your personal best is stored locally in your browser using local storage. It may not transfer to another device or browser.
