About Escape Room

Escape Room is a free online puzzle adventure game where players solve riddles, logic questions and mystery challenges to unlock new rooms. Start with Easy mode, progress through Medium and Hard, and try to complete all 15 rooms before the countdown reaches zero.

How to Play

Read the mission story and puzzle carefully. Type your answer in the answer box and press Unlock Room. Use hints if you get stuck, but remember that each hint costs points. Complete all five rooms in a difficulty level to unlock the next one.
