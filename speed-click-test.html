What is a Speed Click Test?

A Speed Click Test measures how many times you can click within a selected time limit. Your result is measured in CPS, which means Clicks Per Second. This simple online game lets you challenge your clicking speed on a computer, tablet or mobile phone.

How to Play

  • Select a test duration: 5, 10 or 30 seconds.
  • Press the Start Test button.
  • Tap or click the large button as quickly as you comfortably can.
  • Watch your score and live CPS.
  • Check your final result and try to beat your personal best.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What does CPS mean?

CPS stands for Clicks Per Second. It represents the average number of clicks made per second during your test.

2. Can I play on mobile?

Yes. The game supports touch input on smartphones and tablets, as well as mouse clicks on desktop computers.

3. Is this Speed Click Test free?

Yes. You can play the test for free without creating an account.

4. Does the game save my best score?

Yes. Your personal best is saved in your browser using local storage. It may not transfer to another device or browser.
