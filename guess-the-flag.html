About Guess The Flag Quiz

Guess The Flag is a free geography game that challenges you to identify countries from their national flags. Improve your knowledge of world geography while enjoying a quick and interactive quiz.

How to Play

  • Choose Easy, Medium, Hard or Expert difficulty.
  • Look at the displayed national flag.
  • Select the correct country from four options.
  • Answer before the countdown reaches zero.
  • Use hints, 50:50 and skip when needed.
  • Complete 15 questions and check your result.

Scoring System

Correct answers earn 100 base points, with a small time bonus for answering quickly. Consecutive correct answers can also earn a streak bonus. Incorrect answers and skipped questions do not earn points.
