How to Calculate Percentage?

A percentage represents a number as a fraction of 100. It is commonly used to calculate exam results, discounts, marks, statistics and more.

1. Exam Marks Percentage Formula

Percentage = (Obtained Marks ÷ Total Marks) × 100

Example: If you score 425 marks out of 500, your percentage is: (425 ÷ 500) × 100 = 85%.

2. Find a Percentage of a Number

Result = (Number × Percentage) ÷ 100

Example: To find 20% of 500, calculate (500 × 20) ÷ 100. The answer is 100.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate my exam marks percentage?

Divide your obtained marks by the total marks and multiply the answer by 100. For example, 450 out of 500 is 90%.

How do I find 20% of a number?

Multiply the number by 20 and divide the result by 100. For example, 20% of 500 is 100.
