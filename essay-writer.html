Tips for Writing a Better Essay

  • Understand the topic before starting.
  • Use a clear introduction to explain the subject.
  • Organize the main ideas into meaningful paragraphs.
  • Use reliable sources to verify facts and statistics.
  • Write a conclusion that summarizes your main points.
  • Proofread the final draft for grammar, clarity and accuracy.
  • Add your own analysis and examples instead of relying entirely on generated text.
