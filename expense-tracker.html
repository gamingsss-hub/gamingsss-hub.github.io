Free Online Expense Tracker

Toolora AI Expense Tracker helps you record everyday spending in one place. Add an expense, choose its category and date, and review your spending totals. You can also set a monthly budget and export your records as a CSV file for your own reference.

How to Use the Expense Tracker

  • Enter a name and amount for your expense.
  • Select a category and transaction date.
  • Press Add Transaction to save the record.
  • Set a monthly budget to compare spending with your target.
  • Use search and category filters to find specific expenses.
  • Export your records as a CSV file whenever needed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this expense tracker free?

Yes. All features on this page are free to use.

Where is my expense data stored?

Your records are saved in your browser's local storage on this device. They are not uploaded to a Toolora account or server.

Can I set a monthly budget?

Yes. Enter a budget amount and the tracker will compare it with expenses dated in the current month.

Can I download my expense history?

Yes. Use Export CSV to download your expense records and open them in compatible spreadsheet software.

Will my data appear on another device?

No. Browser local storage is specific to the browser and device. Your records do not automatically sync between devices.
