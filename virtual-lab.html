Understand The Science

Learn the important physics concepts behind your experiments.

Electric Current

Electric current is the rate of flow of electric charge through a conductor. Its SI unit is the ampere (A).

I = Q / t

Voltage & Resistance

Voltage is the electrical potential difference between two points. Resistance describes opposition to current flow.

V = I × R

Acceleration

Acceleration is the rate of change of velocity with time. It can be positive, negative or zero.

a = (v - u) / t

Velocity

Velocity describes the rate of change of displacement. It has both magnitude and direction.

v = displacement / time

Focal Length

Focal length is the distance between the optical centre of a lens and its principal focus.

f = focal length

Image Formation

A convex lens can form real or virtual images depending on the position of the object.

1/f = 1/v + 1/u

Physics In Real Life

Discover where these scientific principles are used in everyday technology.

Electric Circuits

Phones, computers, lights and household appliances use electrical circuits to operate.

Motion & Vehicles

Acceleration and velocity help engineers understand vehicle movement, braking and safety.

Optics & Cameras

Camera lenses focus light to create images. Similar optical principles are used in microscopes and telescopes.

Virtual Lab Safety & Learning Note

These are educational computer simulations, not physical laboratory equipment. Real experiments should only be performed with suitable supervision, approved equipment and proper safety procedures. Never connect a real circuit to mains electricity as part of an unsupervised experiment.
