Free Online Calculator

Toolora AI Calculator is a free browser-based math tool for everyday calculations. Use it to add, subtract, multiply and divide numbers, work with percentages, or solve expressions using brackets. No account or installation is required.

How to Use This Calculator

  • Click the number buttons to enter your calculation.
  • Select an operator such as addition, subtraction, multiplication or division.
  • Use brackets to group parts of an expression.
  • Press the equals button to calculate the answer.
  • Use DEL to remove the last character or AC to clear everything.
  • Click a previous result in Recent History to restore it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this online calculator free?

Yes. You can use all calculator features for free without creating an account.

Can I use my keyboard?

Yes. Number keys and arithmetic operators are supported. Press Enter to calculate, Backspace to delete and Escape to clear.

Does this calculator support percentages?

Yes. Enter a number and press the percentage button to divide the current value by 100. For example, 25% becomes 0.25.

Is my calculation data uploaded?

No. Calculations run locally in your browser. This page does not send your expressions to a calculation server.
